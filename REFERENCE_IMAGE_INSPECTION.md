## Consolidated visual system extracted from all references

The supplied references repeatedly use square 1:1 cards, with a smaller number of landscape cards. The strongest recurring patterns are full-bleed lifestyle photographs with dark gradients, soft duotone/blurred backgrounds, image-safe negative space, thin gradient or single-color borders, large quote marks, centered or left-aligned quote blocks, bold sans-serif or italic/script serif typography, selective keyword highlighting, category/title labels, decorative metaphors, and compact source/brand footers. Logos and identity are placed in several predictable zones: upper-left or upper-right publisher mark, centered logo below the quote, lower-left website/brand, lower-right social handle, and separate Facebook/Instagram handle corners.

A production template system should therefore expose structured fields rather than generate one fixed motivational image: template family, canvas ratio, background media, overlay/gradient, focal-safe area, border, quote mark, title/category label, quote text, highlighted spans, decorative asset, logo position, website, phone/WhatsApp CTA, address/location, Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn handles, QR code/booking CTA, and footer visibility. For a fashion brand, these same primitives should be repurposed for product photography and catalog cards, not used to force irrelevant motivational content.

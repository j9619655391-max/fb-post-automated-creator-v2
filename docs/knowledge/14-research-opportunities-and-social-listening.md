# Research, Opportunities, and Social-Listening Study

## Purpose

A static quote page can stay visually consistent while its ideas become stale. The research and opportunity layer discovers timely themes, audience questions, and relevant public signals that can inspire new content without forcing unrelated news or unsupported claims into the page.

## Opportunity lifecycle

| State | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Discovered | A public signal or idea was found | Store source, timestamp, topic, and confidence |
| Assessed | Relevance and risk were evaluated | Map to workspace/category or discard |
| Approved for brief | Operator accepted the idea as a content direction | Convert into a creative brief |
| Used | A draft/package was created from it | Link opportunity to content/audit |
| Stale | Too old or no longer relevant | Do not reuse automatically |
| Rejected | Wrong fit, unsafe, or unsupported | Preserve reason for learning |

## Source hierarchy

Client-provided workspace context and approved first-party sources outrank public commentary. Public news, research, and trend signals should be labeled with source, date, and confidence. A signal may inspire a question or theme, but factual claims require verification before appearing in an image or caption.

## Quote-page application

For Love, Truth, Motivational, and Pain categories, research should produce relatable angles rather than generic “viral” copying. Examples include a reflective question, a documented public idea, a seasonal emotional context, or a language/culture insight. The system must not convert tragedy, health claims, political events, or sensitive personal experiences into clickbait motivational content.

## Social listening boundaries

Collect only public, relevant signals needed for topic discovery and performance learning. Do not infer a person’s mental health, identity, or vulnerability from comments. Aggregate repeated questions or themes at the workspace level. Respect platform terms, access controls, rate limits, and source attribution requirements.

## Opportunity-to-brief contract

Each approved opportunity should produce a brief containing source reference, relevance explanation, category, audience, language mode, claim boundary, creative direction, and expiry. The operator sees this context before generation. The system must not silently convert a trend into an external post.

## Tests

Test source status, stale opportunity handling, workspace relevance, claim grounding, category mapping, duplicate opportunities, public-data minimization, and the rule that discovery never triggers image generation or publishing automatically.

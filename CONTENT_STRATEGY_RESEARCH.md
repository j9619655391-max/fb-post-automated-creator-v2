
# Content strategy research

## Organic post versus advertisement

Meta's official guidance states that a boosted post is an ad created from existing Facebook Page or Instagram content, and that it is still considered an ad because it requires a budget to reach a wider audience. Meta distinguishes this from an organic post that is published without a paid promotion budget. Meta also states that Ads Manager provides more advanced objectives, placements, targeting, creative controls, budgets, and performance metrics than a basic boosted post.

Sources:

1. Meta for Business, "The difference between boosted posts and Meta ads": https://www.facebook.com/business/help/317083072148603
2. Meta for Business, "About boosted posts": https://www.facebook.com/business/help/240208966080581
3. Meta for Business, "Boost a post from your Facebook Page": https://www.facebook.com/business/help/347839548598012

Product implication: the platform must label and separate `organic post`, `boost recommendation`, and `paid campaign/ad creative`. Generating a branded image or publishing an organic post does not itself create an advertisement. It becomes paid advertising only after an operator chooses a paid objective, audience, budget, placements, duration, and publishes/boosts through the advertising system.
